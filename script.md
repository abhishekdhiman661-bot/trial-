# Script: Do We Really Only Use 10% Of Our Brain? 🧠

**Format**: 3D Vertical Short (Zack D Films style)  
**Language**: Hinglish (Calm, flat narrator tone)  
**Target Runtime**: ~34–37 seconds  
**Word Count**: ~118 words (8 scenes)

---

## Narration Lines (Recording Teleprompter)

> [!TIP]
> **Delivery Tip**: Zack D style narration bilkul **calm, steady aur matter-of-fact** tone mein bola jata hai. Har line ke baad lagbhag 0.5s ka natural pause rakhein.

1. **Line 1 (Cold Open - 0 to 4s)**:  
   *Aapne zaroor suna hoga ki insaan dimaag ka sirf 10 percent use karta hai, lekin yeh bilkul jhooth hai.*

2. **Line 2 (Science Pivot - 4 to 9s)**:  
   *Brain scans dikhate hain ki simple baat karte waqt ya sote hue bhi, poora dimaag active rehta hai.*

3. **Line 3 (Energy Metric - 9 to 14s)**:  
   *Dimaag body ka sirf do percent weight leta hai, par poore shareer ki 20 percent energy akele consume karta hai.*

4. **Line 4 (Evolutionary Logic - 14 to 18s)**:  
   *Agar 90 percent dimaag sach mein bekaar hota, toh nature aisi useless energy waste kabhi allow nahi karti.*

5. **Line 5 (Clinical Reality - 18 to 22s)**:  
   *Medical reality yeh hai ki agar dimaag ke kisi ek percent chhotey hisse par bhi chot lag jaye,*

6. **Line 6 (Severe Impact - 22 to 26s)**:  
   *toh insaan apni bolne ya chalne ki ability hamesha ke liye kho sakta hai.*

7. **Line 7 (Myth Origin - 26 to 30s)**:  
   *Yeh myth bas ek purani research ko galat samajhne ki wajah se faila tha.*

8. **Line 8 (Deadpan Loop Payoff - 30 to 35s)**:  
   *Toh sach yeh hai ki aap abhi bhi apna poora dimaag use kar rahe hain, chahe aapko aisa feel ho ya na ho.*
